Privacy Policy / 日本語

How to play

Look at the scene and tap one of three items. Keep choosing correctly to clear the stage. Two misses ends the stage. There are 10 stories, and clearing one opens the next. The in-game text is in Japanese.

About ads

Stories 1 and 2 have no ads. From story 3 on, full-screen ads appear at breaks between stories and when you tap retry after losing all your lives. Video ads play only when you tap a button. There are no purchases.

About the tracking prompt

The prompt on first launch asks whether ads may be matched to you. Every story is playable with either answer. You can change it later in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security, Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you will also see a consent message about the use of data for ads.

Playing offline

You can play without a connection. Ads will not appear, and video buttons may not work.
