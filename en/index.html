Save the Poor Family

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 / Revised November 2026, applies from version 1.0.17 / 日本語 / Support

This policy explains how mino works handles information in the app Save the Poor Family.

Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect names, email addresses, precise location or similar personal data. The app has no login and no developer server.

Information sent for advertising

The app uses Google AdMob, an ad service of Google LLC. Google components built into the app may collect the following and send it to Google in order to show ads:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your permission) and identifiers scoped to the app or developer
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • Advertising data: ads shown, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic data: crash logs and performance data

The app uses the network only to show and verify ads. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner sites.

Tracking permission

On the first launch after install, and on the next launch after updating from an earlier version, the app explains ads and then shows the iOS permission prompt. If you allow it, you may see ads based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, every story is still playable; ads still appear but may be less relevant. You can change this later in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

In these regions the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent to the use of data for ads. You can play without consenting. In that case, ads are limited to those allowed by law. To choose again at any time, tap Change ad consent on the title screen.

Stored on your device

Cleared stages, stars, story progress, your answers to the permission prompts, a record used to space out ads, whether the rating prompt was shown, and whether Remove Ads was purchased are saved on your device. Game progress is never sent elsewhere. It is removed when you delete the app.

Where ads appear, and analytics

Stories 1 and 2 have no ads. From story 3 on, full-screen ads appear at breaks between stories and when you tap retry after losing all your lives. Video ads play only when you tap a button. The developer does not run usage analytics. If this changes, this page will be updated before release.

Remove Ads purchase

The app offers a one-time purchase, Remove Ads. Payment and purchase records are handled by Apple, and the developer does not receive payment information. The app only saves a mark on your device that the purchase was made, and never sends it elsewhere. After purchase, full-screen ads and the ad before a retry no longer appear, and you can continue after losing all lives without watching an ad. The app stops loading ads. On a new or reinstalled device, tap 購入を復元 (Restore Purchase) on the title screen.

Children

The developer does not collect personal data from anyone of any age. Ad content follows Google's advertising policies.
