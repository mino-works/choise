Save the Poor Family

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 / Revised November 19, 2026, gameplay analytics added / 日本語 / Support

This policy explains how mino works handles information in the app Save the Poor Family.

Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect names, email addresses, precise location or similar personal data. The app has no login and no developer server.

Information sent for advertising

The app uses Google AdMob, an ad service of Google LLC. Google components built into the app may collect the following and send it to Google in order to show ads:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only with your permission) and identifiers scoped to the app or developer
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • Advertising data: ads shown, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic data: crash logs and performance data

The app uses the network only to show and verify ads, and for the gameplay analytics below. See the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner sites.

Tracking permission

On the first launch after install, and on the next launch after updating from an earlier version, the app explains ads and then shows the iOS permission prompt. If you allow it, you may see ads based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, every story is still playable; ads still appear but may be less relevant. You can change this later in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

In these regions the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent to the use of data for ads. You can play without consenting. In that case, ads are limited to those allowed by law. To choose again at any time, tap Change ad consent on the title screen. Gameplay analytics follows the same choice. If you do not consent to storing information on your device, or until you answer, no gameplay analytics are collected.

Stored on your device

Cleared stages, stars, story progress, your answers to the permission prompts, a record used to space out ads, whether the rating prompt was shown, whether the notification permission was asked, and whether Remove Ads was purchased are saved on your device. Your saved progress itself is not sent elsewhere. Only the counts described under Gameplay analytics are sent. It is removed when you delete the app.

Notifications

Only if you allow it, the app schedules reminders about the next story on your device. They are not sent from a server, and no information leaves your device. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

Where ads appear

Stories 1 and 2 have no ads. From story 3 on, full-screen ads appear at breaks between stories and when you tap retry after losing all your lives. Video ads play only when you tap a button.

Gameplay analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google LLC, to count which story you start, which question you miss, which card you choose, whether you answer correctly, clear or quit a story, and whether you open the app from the title screen or a reminder. This is used only to improve the stories and puzzles. Your name, email address, the advertising identifier IDFA and the vendor identifier IDFV are not sent. What is sent is a number created for each app install, the device model and OS version, a general location such as country or region estimated from the IP address, and the fact, product and price of a Remove Ads purchase. The developer does not link this to you and does not use it for ads or tracking. See also Firebase privacy and security at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Remove Ads purchase

The app offers a one-time purchase, Remove Ads. Payment and purchase records are handled by Apple, and the developer does not receive payment information. The app saves a mark on your device that the purchase was made. The purchase is counted as described under Gameplay analytics, but no payment information is sent. After purchase, full-screen ads and the ad before a retry no longer appear, and you can continue after losing all lives without watching an ad. The app stops loading ads. On a new or reinstalled device, tap 購入を復元 (Restore Purchase) on the title screen.

Children

The developer does not collect personal data from anyone of any age. Ad content follows Google's advertising policies.
